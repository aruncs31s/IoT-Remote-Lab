Welcome to IoT Remote Lab

IoT Remote Lab is a powerful web-based platform designed for remote programming, monitoring, and management of ESP32 and ESP8266 IoT devices. Whether you're a student, educator, or developer, this tool allows you to:

  • Connect Devices: Easily detect and connect to ESP devices via USB
  • Program Remotely: Upload Arduino code to your devices without physical access
  • Monitor in Real-Time: View serial output and device status instantly
  • Simulate Projects: Test your IoT ideas in a virtual environment
  • Manage Multiple Devices: Handle several ESP devices from one interface

Get started by connecting your ESP device and exploring the features!
